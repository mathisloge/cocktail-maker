= Contracts

A contract states what crosses one boundary between two blocks or parties of the machine, and what each side guarantees to the other.
Each contract has an ID of the form `IF-nn`, and each of its requirements has an ID of the form `IF-nn-Rnn`.
The IDs are stable and never reused.
Other pages and the subprojects refer to a contract or a requirement by its ID.
The boundaries come from the blocks of xref:building-blocks.adoc[chapter 5].
