= Diagram Conventions

Every diagram on this site uses the same shapes, colours and line styles.
This page explains them, so it is the legend of every diagram.

.The shapes and edges of every diagram

}

  class: boundary
  power: "power-part · supplies power" {class: power-part}

What does each shape and each edge of a diagram mean?

Colour is never the only cue.
The label of an edge says the same, and for data and mechanical edges the dashes do too.

== Edges

The colour and the line style of an edge show its kind.
The meanings are the same in every diagram of every component.

[cols="2,3,4,3,2,4", options="header"]
|===
| Sample | Edge class | Meaning | Colour | Hex | Line style

| [.edge-sample.edge-power]#&#8203;#
| `power`
| electrical power
| orange red
| `#d0654a`
| solid, width 2

| [.edge-sample.edge-data]#&#8203;#
| `data`
| data and communication
| light slate
| `#86aebd`
| dashed, width 2

| [.edge-sample.edge-fluid]#&#8203;#
| `fluid`
| liquid flow
| green
| `#5cad75`
| solid, width 2

| [.edge-sample.edge-mechanical]#&#8203;#
| `mechanical`
| a physical, mechanical link
| violet
| `#8c7ee0`
| short dashes, width 2

| [.edge-sample.edge-interaction]#&#8203;#
| `interaction`
| a person using the machine, or the machine telling or asking a person
| light amber
| `#e8b86d`
| solid, width 2

| [.edge-sample.edge-fault]#&#8203;#
| `fault`
| an actual fault or an error path, such as a timeout
| pink
| `#e07aa8`
| solid, width 2

| [.edge-sample.edge-normal]#&#8203;#
| `normal`
| a connection without a kind
| muted sand
| `#867f6b`
| solid, width 1

| [.edge-sample.edge-data.edge-key]#&#8203;#
| `key`
| the one highlighted connection of a diagram, added to its kind
| the colour of its kind
| none
| width 3 and a bold label
|===

== Shapes

The class of a shape follows what the shape is in its diagram.
A part class colours only the outline, and every outline is solid.

[cols="2,4,6,3,2", options="header"]
|===
| Sample | Shape class | Put it on | Outline | Hex

| [.shape-sample.shape-actor]#&#8203;#
| `actor`
| a person, such as the Guest or the Operator
| light amber
| `#e8b86d`

| [.shape-sample.shape-external]#&#8203;#
| `external`
| a system outside the subject of the diagram, such as the Power Grid
| sand, dashed
| `#d9cfae`

| [.shape-sample.shape-boundary]#&#8203;#
| `boundary`
| the frame of the machine or of a subproject, a group, or a note in a sequence diagram
| amber, width 2
| `#c8913a`

| [.shape-sample.shape-block]#&#8203;#
| `block`
| a building block without a specific area, such as the Station or a Pod
| amber
| `#c8913a`

| [.shape-sample.shape-power-part]#&#8203;#
| `power-part`
| a part that supplies power, such as the mains inlet, the main switch, a power supply or a DC/DC converter
| orange red
| `#d0654a`

| [.shape-sample.shape-data-part]#&#8203;#
| `data-part`
| a computer, a controller or other electronics, such as the Raspberry Pi, the Pod controller or a stepper driver
| light slate
| `#86aebd`

| [.shape-sample.shape-fluid-part]#&#8203;#
| `fluid-part`
| a part that carries liquid, such as the fluid hub or the dispenser unit
| green
| `#5cad75`

| [.shape-sample.shape-mechanical-part]#&#8203;#
| `mechanical-part`
| an actuator or a mechanical part, such as a stepper motor, a pinch valve or a load cell
| violet
| `#8c7ee0`

| [.shape-sample.shape-interaction-part]#&#8203;#
| `interaction-part`
| a part that a person uses, such as the touch display
| light amber
| `#e8b86d`
|===
