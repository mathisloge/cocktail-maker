= Fault Scenarios

This page shows what happens across the whole machine when a fault occurs.
Each scenario names the contracts that its steps rely on, and lists what is still open.
Normal operation is in xref:runtime-view.adoc[chapter 6].

[[link-breaks-during-pour]]
== The Link to a Pod Breaks During a Pour

The USB link to a Pod breaks while the Pod pours, and the end state is open.
The Station fails the mix and sends an `EmergencyStop` to every Pod, but nobody has decided whether the Pod that lost its link ends its pour.

What does the machine do when the link to a Pod breaks during a pour, and what does the Pod do?

=== Contracts
* xref:contracts.adoc#IF-01[IF-01], xref:contracts.adoc#IF-01-R06[IF-01-R06]: The Station notices a Pod that does not answer within a bounded time, and the other Pods and the UI are not stalled.
* xref:contracts.adoc#IF-01-R07[IF-01-R07]: The Station can stop every pump and valve of a Pod at once, and it does so for every Pod.
* xref:contracts.adoc#IF-01-R11[IF-01-R11]: A Pod should end a running pour on its own when the link breaks, which is open.
* xref:contracts.adoc#IF-07[IF-07], xref:contracts.adoc#IF-07-R03[IF-07-R03]: A pulled or pinched cable never leaves a Pod pouring, which is open.

=== Open
* Whether a Pod ends a running pour on its own when the USB link breaks, and who guarantees it, because the only known bound is the limit of 30 s for a pour (IF-01-R11, IF-07-R03).
* Which bounded time the Station needs to notice a silent Pod, and what it does with a Pod that stays silent and with the requests that are pending for it (IF-01-R06).
* Whether the `EmergencyStop` reaches the Pod with the broken link, and what that Pod does when it does not (IF-01-R07).

=== Details
* xref:contracts.adoc#IF-01[IF-01], section Fault behaviour: every Pod exchange has its own timeout, so the Station notices a silent Pod within a bounded time, and how the request fails is open.
* xref:station::runtime-view.adoc#_a_guest_mixes_a_drink[Station runtime view, A Guest Mixes a Drink]: a failed dispense stops the remaining actions and sends an `EmergencyStop` to every Pod.
* xref:protocol.adoc#_reply_times[Protocol page, reply times]: the timeouts of the Station.

[[power-fails-during-pour]]
== The Power Fails During a Pour

The power fails during a pour, and the end state is an unfinished drink in the glass.
The Station and every Pod stop at once, and when the power returns, the Station starts again from its state files and corrects the fill levels.
What a Pod actuator does at that moment is open.

----

pmu -> station: "stops the 5 V DC\n(open, IF-02)" {class: power}
pmu -> pod: "stops the 24 V DC,\nthe pump stops with it" {class: [power; key]}
station.files: "The state files on disk are whole,\nbecause each is written in one step" {class: boundary}
pod.calibration: "Calibrations stay in the Pod,\nthe load cell forgets its zero point" {class: boundary}
power_grid -> pmu: "supplies 230 V AC again" {class: power}
pmu -> station: "supplies 5 V DC again" {class: power}
pmu -> pod: "supplies 24 V DC again,\nno actuator may move before\nthe Station starts the Pod\n(open, IF-03-R04)" {class: power}

----
What happens to a running pour when the power fails, and how does the machine come back?

=== Contracts
* xref:contracts.adoc#IF-04[IF-04]: The Power Grid supplies the 230 V AC, and a mains of 230 V AC at 50 Hz is the only one the machine takes.
* xref:contracts.adoc#IF-02[IF-02], xref:contracts.adoc#IF-02-R03[IF-02-R03]: A power cut at any moment leaves the Station able to start again with its data intact.
* xref:contracts.adoc#IF-03[IF-03], xref:contracts.adoc#IF-03-R04[IF-03-R04]: All Pods take their power from the one 24 V rail, and no actuator should move before the Station has started the Pod again.
* xref:contracts.adoc#IF-01-R01[IF-01-R01]: A Pod never acts without a request from the Station, so it does not go on with the pour by itself.

=== Open
* Whether the Station gets a warning or a short run-on time before the 5 V stops, and what it does on a brown-out of the 5 V (IF-02).
* What a Pod does when the 24 V drops or returns during a pour, and who guarantees that no actuator moves before the Station has started the Pod (IF-03-R04).

=== Details
* xref:station::runtime-view.adoc#_a_pod_comes_back_after_a_power_loss[Station runtime view, A Pod Comes Back After a Power Loss]: how the Station corrects the fill levels after the load cells took a new zero point.
* xref:runtime-view.adoc#switching-on[Switching On]: how the machine starts and how the Station finds its Pods.
* xref:station:adr:006-nixos-deployment.adoc[STA-006]: once the Station is deployed with NixOS, the NixOS configuration will start the Station program after the boot, so the Station will start by itself when the power returns.
* xref:adr:004-power-architecture.adoc[SYS-004]: the single power supply is a single point of failure, so a power supply fault ends the same way.

[[guest-cancels]]
== A Guest Cancels

A Guest cancels a running drink, and the end state is that every pump and valve of every Pod has stopped and the Guest is on a known-good screen.
A Pod that does not acknowledge the stop leaves this end state open.

----

no_ack: "opt: A Pod does not acknowledge the stop" {
  class: boundary
  station -> pod: "gets no acknowledgement,\nthe pour ends after 30 s at the latest\n(open, IF-01-R11)" {class: fault}

----
What does a Guest set off by cancelling a drink, and what stops where?

=== Contracts
* xref:contracts.adoc#IF-01[IF-01], xref:contracts.adoc#IF-01-R07[IF-01-R07]: The Station can stop every pump and valve of a Pod at once.
* xref:contracts.adoc#IF-01-R06[IF-01-R06]: A Pod that does not answer is noticed within a bounded time, and it stalls neither another Pod nor the UI.
* xref:contracts.adoc#IF-01-R11[IF-01-R11]: A Pod should end a running pour on its own when the link breaks, which is open.
* xref:contracts.adoc#IF-08[IF-08]: The `EmergencyStop` stops every pump and valve, and a running tare or load cell calibration carries on.

=== Open
* What the Station does when a Pod does not acknowledge the `EmergencyStop`, and whether the Guest or the Operator is told, because the sources decide nothing more than the limit of 30 s for a pour (IF-01-R11).

=== Details
* xref:station::runtime-view.adoc#_a_guest_mixes_a_drink[Station runtime view, A Guest Mixes a Drink]: a cancel stops the remaining actions and sends an `EmergencyStop` to every Pod.
* xref:protocol.adoc#_request_acknowledgement[Protocol page, request and acknowledgement]: every `Dispense` and `DispenserStartCalibration` that is running ends with a `Nak`, and a running tare or load cell calibration carries on.
* The Guest can also cancel in the refill dialog, and the Station then stops the Pods in the same way (see <<bottle-empty-during-pour>>).

[[bottle-empty-during-pour]]
== A Bottle Runs Empty During a Pour

A bottle runs empty during a pour, and the end state is a finished drink after a refill, or an ended drink after a cancel.
A blocked line looks the same as an empty bottle, so the Station only suspects that the bottle is empty, and a cancel in the dialog ends the drink instead.

What does the machine do when a bottle runs empty during a pour, and who has to act?

=== Contracts
* xref:contracts.adoc#IF-05[IF-05], xref:contracts.adoc#IF-05-R05[IF-05-R05]: An empty bottle is noticed, and its missing amount is never counted as poured.
* xref:contracts.adoc#IF-01-R08[IF-01-R08]: The Pod reports the amount that really came out, and a pour that came up short is never reported as complete.
* xref:contracts.adoc#IF-05-R03[IF-05-R03]: An Operator refills a bottle from the top while the Pod keeps weighing it.
* xref:contracts.adoc#IF-08[IF-08], xref:contracts.adoc#IF-08-R03[IF-08-R03]: The Operator reaches every point that service needs with the Pod installed.

=== Open
* How the Operator reaches the bottle during a drink, and whether the Pod must be opened for it (IF-08-R03).
* The Station does not notice an Operator who puts the wrong ingredient into the bottle (IF-05-R02).

=== Details
* xref:station::runtime-view.adoc#_a_bottle_runs_empty_and_gets_refilled[Station runtime view, A Bottle Runs Empty and Gets Refilled]: the dialog without PIN, the wait of 1 s, the rise of 50 g and the dry run of about 6.5 s when someone confirms without a refill.
* xref:station::runtime-view.adoc#_a_guest_mixes_a_drink[Station runtime view, A Guest Mixes a Drink]: which pour results count as an empty bottle.
* xref:protocol.adoc#_request_acknowledgement_event[Protocol page, request, acknowledgement and event]: the `BottleEmpty` result of the `DispenseFinished` event.
* <<guest-cancels>>: what a cancel sets off.
* xref:runtime-view.adoc#refilling[Refilling in chapter 6]: how an Operator refills outside a drink.

[[pump-lid-opened]]
== The Pump Lid Is Opened While the Pump Can Move

The end state is open, because the pump has no interlock: opening the lid does not stop the pump, and a finger can be trapped when the pump can still move.
This is the residual risk that IF-08 states, and the lid, the rule never to run the pump without the lid and the service steps are the precaution.

  class: boundary
  operator -> pod: "removes the lid screws\nand pulls the lid off" {class: interaction}
  pod -> operator: "pump keeps moving, the nip between roller\nand tube can trap a finger,\nno interlock stops it\n(open, IF-08-R04)" {class: [fault; key]}
}

What stops the pump when an Operator opens its lid, and what does the machine leave to the Operator?

=== Contracts
* xref:contracts.adoc#IF-08[IF-08], xref:contracts.adoc#IF-08-R01[IF-08-R01]: An Operator cannot reach a moving part or a pinch point while a Pod can still move, and the lid is the guard of the pump.
* xref:contracts.adoc#IF-08-R04[IF-08-R04]: An Operator can bring every actuator of a Pod to rest before opening it, which is open.
* xref:contracts.adoc#IF-01-R07[IF-01-R07]: The Station can stop every pump and valve of a Pod at once.
* xref:contracts.adoc#IF-01-R01[IF-01-R01]: A Pod never acts without a request from the Station.

=== Open
* What brings every actuator of a Pod to rest when an Operator opens it, because the pump has no interlock, and how the Operator stops the pump before the service steps (IF-08-R04).

=== Details
* xref:mechanics::pump.adoc[Mechanics pump page]: the warning never to run the pump without the lid, because the nip between roller and tube can trap a finger.
* xref:mechanics::pump-service.adoc[Mechanics pump service page]: the tube change starts with stopping the pump and draining the line, then the three lid screws.
* xref:mechanics:adr:002-pump-fixed-occlusion-bed.adoc[CM3D-002]: the pump has no interlock.
* xref:mechanics:adr:004-pump-closed-housing-bearing-lid.adoc[CM3D-004]: the lid of the closed housing is the guard.
