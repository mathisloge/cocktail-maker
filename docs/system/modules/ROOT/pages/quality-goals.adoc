= Quality Goals

To ensure the machine operates safely and reliably in real-world scenarios, all architectural and design decisions are evaluated against the following strict quality criteria.
Safety and reliability are the primary drivers for both hardware separation and software state management.
The order of the table is the priority.
The IDs are stable.
Other pages and ADRs refer to a goal by its ID.
The goals carry tags of the arc42 quality model Q42 (xref:adr:014-quality-model-q42.adoc[SYS-014]), shown in the last column.
The safety argument for CE rests on the risk assessment.
Chapter 10 breaks the goals down into quality scenarios and lists further goals, see xref:quality-requirements.adoc[].

[cols="1,2,5,3", options="header"]
|===
| ID | Quality Goal | Scenario | Q42

| [[QG-01]]QG-01
| *Human Safety*
| No Guest and no Operator is harmed by the machine, in use or in service, not by a mechanism, an electrical fault or a software bug.
| `#safe`

| [[QG-02]]QG-02
| *Food Safety*
| Every drink contains only the ingredients of its recipe in the right amounts, and only food-safe parts touch it.
| `#safe`, `#suitable`

| [[QG-03]]QG-03
| *Reliability*
| The machine serves drinks through a whole event without an Operator having to restart it, and a failing Pod stops the drink on every Pod.
| `#reliable`

| [[QG-04]]QG-04
| *Serviceability*
| An Operator with a short briefing refills, swaps a bottle, changes a pump tube and calibrates, using only the written steps.
| `#operable`, `#secure`

| [[QG-05]]QG-05
| *Usability*
| A Guest who has never used the machine orders a drink without help.
| `#usable`, `#efficient`
|===
