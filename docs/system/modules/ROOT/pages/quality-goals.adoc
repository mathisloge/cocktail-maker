= Quality Goals

To ensure the machine operates safely and reliably in real-world scenarios, all architectural and design decisions are evaluated against the following strict quality criteria.
Safety and reliability are the primary drivers for both hardware separation and software state management.
The IDs are stable.
Other pages and ADRs refer to a goal by its ID.
The goals map onto the quality model of ISO/IEC 25010:2023, shown in the last column, and the quality tree of chapter 10 will build on that model.

[cols="1,2,5,3", options="header"]
|===
| ID | Quality Goal | Description | ISO 25010:2023

| [[QG-01]]QG-01
| *Human Safety*
| No mechanism, electrical fault, or software bug may endanger a Guest or an Operator.
| Safety (fail safe, hazard warning, operational constraint)

| [[QG-02]]QG-02
| *Food Safety*
| All parts that touch a person or a drink, and all flow paths, use food-safe materials, and no wrong ingredient or quantity reaches a drink.
| Safety (operational constraint, fail safe)

| [[QG-03]]QG-03
| *Reliability*
| The machine must run unattended for long stretches without failure.
| Reliability (faultlessness, availability, fault tolerance)

| [[QG-04]]QG-04
| *Serviceability*
| Operator can service, refill, and calibrate without deep expertise.
| Interaction capability (operability, learnability)

| [[QG-05]]QG-05
| *Usability*
| Guests with no instructions can order and receive a drink intuitively.
| Interaction capability (appropriateness recognizability, learnability, operability)

| [[QG-06]]QG-06
| *Maintainability*
| Developers can change, test and verify the code, the firmware and the hardware design with little effort.
| Maintainability (modularity, modifiability, testability)

|===
