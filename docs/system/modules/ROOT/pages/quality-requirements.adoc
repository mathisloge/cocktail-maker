= 10 Quality Requirements

This chapter makes the quality goals measurable.
They are the goals of xref:quality-goals.adoc[] in chapter 1 and the further goal of this chapter.
Each goal has scenarios.
A scenario has a source, a stimulus, a response and a measure, and it has exactly one goal as its parent.
The tags of the arc42 quality model Q42 (xref:adr:014-quality-model-q42.adoc[SYS-014]) are lenses on a goal.
A scenario may also carry tags of other goals, and the table lists all of its tags.

A scenario has one of four states:

* Target: a binding value set for this scenario.
* Provisional: a linked docs page or ADR states the value, and it is still to be confirmed on the assembled machine.
* Open: no value is set yet.
* Withdrawn: the scenario is dropped and keeps its ID.

A scenario is not a safety proof.
The safety argument for CE rests on the risk assessment.
The scenarios of xref:quality-goals.adoc#QG-01[QG-01] and xref:quality-goals.adoc#QG-02[QG-02] are the measurable claims that the risk assessment will refer to.

== Test Setup

The scenarios run on a test setup of the Station and at least 3 Pods on USB.
A scenario that only checks how the Station handles Pods may run against simulated Pods, and its Environment says so.
A scenario about actuators, current, weight or reach runs on real hardware.
A reference instrument is independent of the machine, and its resolution relative to the limit is open.
A count of 0 of N gives only a bound on the rate of a failure and no proof that it cannot happen.

== Scenarios

[[QS-01]]
=== QS-01: A Pod acknowledges an `EmergencyStop` in time

Goal:: xref:quality-goals.adoc#QG-01[QG-01]
Q42:: #safe
Source:: A Guest cancels a running drink, or the Station fails a mix.
Stimulus:: The Station sends an `EmergencyStop` to every Pod.
Artifact:: Station and Pod
Environment:: Normal operation, on real hardware.
Each Pod pours and reads its load cell when the `EmergencyStop` arrives.
Response:: Every Pod acknowledges the `EmergencyStop` to the Station.
Measure:: The time in ms from the write of the `EmergencyStop` at the Station to the read of its `Ack` at the Station, taken from the frames on the link.
The result is the largest time over N stops per Pod, and N is open.
The scenario holds when this value is at most the Value.
The 500 ms allowance of the Reply Times is a timeout of the Station and not a stop time.
Verification:: Test
Value:: none
State:: Open
Checks:: xref:contracts.adoc#IF-01-R07[IF-01-R07], xref:fault-scenarios.adoc#guest-cancels[A Guest Cancels], xref:protocol.adoc#_reply_times[Protocol page, Reply Times]

[[QS-02]]
=== QS-02: All pumps and valves of a Pod come to rest after an `EmergencyStop`

Goal:: xref:quality-goals.adoc#QG-01[QG-01]
Q42:: #safe
Source:: A Guest cancels a running drink, or the Station fails a mix.
Stimulus:: A Pod receives an `EmergencyStop`.
Artifact:: Pod
Environment:: Normal operation, on real hardware, with a pour running in the Pod.

Response:: Every pump of the Pod stands still, no valve moves, and the running pour ends.
Measure:: The time in ms from the arrival of the `EmergencyStop` at the Pod to the last step of a pump or the last movement of a valve.
A step or a movement counts only if none follows for a time T, and T is open.
Both are observed on the Pod, and how a valve movement is observed is open.
The result is the largest time over N stops per Pod, and N is open.
The scenario holds when this value is at most the Value.
Verification:: Test
Value:: none
State:: Open
Checks:: xref:contracts.adoc#IF-01-R07[IF-01-R07], xref:fault-scenarios.adoc#guest-cancels[A Guest Cancels]

[[QS-03]]
=== QS-03: A Pod ends a running pour when the link to the Station breaks

Goal:: xref:quality-goals.adoc#QG-01[QG-01]
Q42:: #safe, #reliable
Source:: The USB link between the Station and a Pod breaks.
Stimulus:: The link breaks while the Pod pours.
Artifact:: Pod
Environment:: Normal operation, on real hardware.
The test pour is longer than the pour limit, so only a reaction of the Pod to the lost link can end it earlier.
The link breaks by a pulled cable.
The pour is in one of three phases at the break: at its start, in its middle and shortly before the pour limit.
A running pour ends at the latest at the pour limit of 30 s (provisional, to be confirmed on the assembled machine).
Whether that limit bounds the stop after a link break is open (IF-01-R11).
Response:: The Pod ends the running pour on its own.
Measure:: The time in s from the last frame that the Pod receives from the Station until every pump of the Pod stands still and no valve moves, with the stop as defined in QS-02.
For a pulled cable the last frame is the cut, seen by an instrument on the link.
The result is the largest time over N breaks per phase, and N is open.
The scenario holds when this value is at most the Value.
Verification:: Test
Value:: none
State:: Open
Checks:: xref:contracts.adoc#IF-01-R11[IF-01-R11], xref:contracts.adoc#IF-07-R03[IF-07-R03], xref:fault-scenarios.adoc#link-breaks-during-pour[The Link to a Pod Breaks During a Pour], xref:protocol.adoc#_reply_times[Protocol page, Reply Times]
