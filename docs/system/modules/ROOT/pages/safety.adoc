= Safety

The power and protocol design directly support the *Human Safety* and *Food Safety* quality goals:

[cols="1,3", options="header"]
|===
| Measure | Supports

| Main switch as a single manual disconnect point
| Human Safety

| RCD/circuit breaker on the AC input stage
| Human Safety

| Continuous PE conductor across all stages
| Human Safety

| Food-safe materials on all parts that touch a person or a drink, and on all flow paths
| Food Safety

| Declared HDPE or PP bottles, emptied and rinsed after at most 48 h (xref:adr:015-food-contact-plastic-bottles.adoc[SYS-015])
| Food Safety

| A liquid of the show front kept away from the Station electronics (open, xref:contracts.adoc#IF-09-R04[IF-09-R04])
| Human Safety
|===
