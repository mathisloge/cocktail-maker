== Context

A Guest sees the touch display and the outside of the Station and the Pods at the same time.
The UI has one dark art-deco theme, and `gui/ui/theme/colors.slint` holds its colours, such as ink, amber, cream and sand.
The docs already derive their palette from the same file.
The parts are printed on a Prusa Core One with INDX, so one part can have several colours (xref:mechanics:adr:003-petg-pump-material.adoc[CM3D-003]).
No rule fixes the colours of the outside today.

=== Decision Drivers

* **A good look:** the machine should look nice, and the screen and the outside should look like one product (QG-05).
* **Food-safe flow path:** a colour must never bring a material into the flow path that is not food-safe (QG-02).

== Quality Goals

* xref:system::quality-goals.adoc#QG-05[QG-05]: The outside repeats the colours of the UI, so the machine looks like one product to the Guest.
* xref:system::quality-goals.adoc#QG-02[QG-02]: The rule covers only the outside, so the parts in the flow path keep their food-safe material.

== Decision

The outside of the Station and the Pods follows the colours in `gui/ui/theme/colors.slint` where possible.

== Alternatives Considered

=== A free colour per part

Each part gets the colour that is at hand or looks good on its own.

Rejected: the machine would look like a collection of parts.

=== A neutral outside

All outside parts are black or grey, independent of the UI.

Rejected: it is simple, but the outside would not match the warm colours of the screen.

== Consequences

* **Good:** The screen and the outside look like one product.
* **Good:** A new part needs no colour decision of its own.
* **Bad:** A filament never matches a palette colour exactly, so the outside only comes close to the screen.
* **Bad:** A change of a UI colour can make printed parts outdated.
* **Bad:** Each colour change in a print costs time and purge material on the INDX.

=== Effect on the Quality Goals

* xref:system::quality-goals.adoc#QG-05[QG-05]: Better, because the screen and the outside use the same colours.
* xref:system::quality-goals.adoc#QG-02[QG-02]: Neutral, because the parts in the flow path are outside the rule.
