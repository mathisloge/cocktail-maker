== Context

Ingredients flow from the bottles through the Pods and the fluid hub of the Station into the glass.
Every part on this path touches food, so it must be food-safe.
Most parts of the Station and the Pods are 3D printed.
The printed parts are not food-safe.
PETG was chosen for the pump parts because of its heat resistance, not for food contact (xref:mechanics:adr:003-petg-pump-material.adoc[CM3D-003]).
The fluid hub and the outlet to the glass are not designed yet.

=== Decision Drivers

* **Food-safe flow path:** no part that touches an ingredient or a drink may be made of a material that is not food-safe (QG-02).
* **Free choice for printed parts:** the material of a printed part should follow its mechanical needs, such as heat and load (QG-06).

== Quality Goals

* xref:system::quality-goals.adoc#QG-02[QG-02]: The food-safety argument rests only on tubes and stainless steel parts, and no printed part is in the flow path.
* xref:system::quality-goals.adoc#QG-06[QG-06]: Printed parts can change material and shape without a food-safety review, but every wetted part must be a tube or a stainless steel part.

== Decision

Only food-safe tubes and stainless steel parts touch an ingredient or a drink.
No 3D printed part touches a liquid.

== Alternatives Considered

=== Printed parts in the flow path

Printed parts such as a fluid hub or an outlet are in direct contact with the liquid.

Rejected: the printed parts are not food-safe.

== Consequences

* **Good:** The food-safety argument covers only a short list of parts, the tubes and the stainless steel parts.
* **Good:** A printed part can change its material or shape without touching the food-safety argument.
* **Bad:** Every part in the flow path, such as the fluid hub, the joints and the outlet, must be a tube or a stainless steel part, which limits its design.

=== Effect on the Quality Goals

* xref:system::quality-goals.adoc#QG-02[QG-02]: Better, because only food-safe tubes and stainless steel parts touch an ingredient or a drink.
* xref:system::quality-goals.adoc#QG-06[QG-06]: Better and worse.
Printed parts are free in their material, but every wetted part is limited to tubes and stainless steel parts.
