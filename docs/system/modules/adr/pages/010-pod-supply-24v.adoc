== Context

The Power Management Unit supplies the Pods from one 24 V DC rail (xref:adr:004-power-architecture.adoc[SYS-004]).
The servos of a Pod need 5 V (xref:ROOT:hardware.adoc[Hardware page]).
The chosen Pod board, the BIGTREETECH Octopus, already converts 24 V down to 5 V.
No page names a part that needs 12 V.

=== Decision Drivers

* **One rail to the Pods:** every voltage that runs to a Pod is another conductor and another fault to guard (QG-01, QG-03).
* **Use what the Pod board has:** the Pod board already makes its 5 V from the 24 V (QG-06).

== Quality Goals

* xref:system::quality-goals.adoc#QG-01[QG-01]: Only one supply voltage runs from the Power Management Unit to a Pod.
* xref:system::quality-goals.adoc#QG-03[QG-03]: A Pod does not depend on a second rail that can fail on its own.
* xref:system::quality-requirements.adoc#QG-06[QG-06]: The Pod design makes its own low voltages without a change to the Station.

== Decision

A Pod takes only 24 V DC as its input and makes the lower voltages it needs, such as the 5 V for the servos, itself.

== Alternatives Considered

=== The Power Management Unit makes 5 V and sends it to the Pods as well

The Power Management Unit sends the 5 V that a Pod needs next to the 24 V.

Rejected: the Pod board already converts 24 V down to 5 V, so a 5 V rail would only add conductors and plugs.

== Consequences

* **Good:** One rail and one plug per Pod for the supply.
* **Good:** A Pod can change its low voltages without a change to the Station or the Power Management Unit.
* **Bad:** A Pod relies on the converter of its board, and another Pod board must also make its 5 V from 24 V.

=== Effect on the Quality Goals

* xref:system::quality-goals.adoc#QG-01[QG-01]: Better, because only one supply voltage runs to a Pod.
* xref:system::quality-goals.adoc#QG-03[QG-03]: Better, because no second rail can fail.
* xref:system::quality-requirements.adoc#QG-06[QG-06]: Better and worse.
The Pod design is free in its low voltages, but another Pod board must also make its 5 V from 24 V.
