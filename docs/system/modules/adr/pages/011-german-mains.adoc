== Context

The machine connects to the external mains through one inlet (xref:adr:004-power-architecture.adoc[SYS-004]).
The Hardware page once named 110 V and 230 V AC for the inlet, while the power page and SYS-004 name 230 V AC.
The machine is designed for the German standard mains.

=== Decision Drivers

* **One mains voltage:** a power supply, a protection and a label for one voltage are simpler to choose (QG-01).

== Quality Goals

* xref:system::quality-goals.adoc#QG-01[QG-01]: The protection and the labels are chosen for one mains voltage.

== Decision

The machine is built for the German standard mains voltage, 230 V AC at 50 Hz, and for no other mains voltage.

== Alternatives Considered

=== 110 V and 230 V AC

The machine accepts both mains voltages.

Rejected: it needs a wide-range power supply and protection for both voltages, and the machine does not need to run on another mains.

== Consequences

* **Good:** The power supply, the protection and the labels are chosen for one voltage.
* **Bad:** The machine is not designed, protected or labelled for another mains voltage.

=== Effect on the Quality Goals

* xref:system::quality-goals.adoc#QG-01[QG-01]: Better, because the electrical protection is designed and checked for one mains voltage.
