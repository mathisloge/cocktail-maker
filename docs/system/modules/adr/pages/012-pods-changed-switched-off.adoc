== Context

A Pod connects to the Station through USB, which is hot-pluggable (xref:adr:002-pysical-pod-connection.adoc[SYS-002]).
A Pod also takes its 24 V supply from the Power Management Unit (xref:adr:010-pod-supply-24v.adoc[SYS-010]).
Connecting or disconnecting a supply conductor under load can cause sparks or a short circuit.

=== Decision Drivers

* **No live work:** an Operator never connects or disconnects a powered supply conductor (QG-01).

== Quality Goals

* xref:system::quality-goals.adoc#QG-01[QG-01]: The Operator connects or disconnects the supply of a Pod only when the machine is off.
* xref:system::quality-goals.adoc#QG-04[QG-04]: Connecting or disconnecting the supply of a Pod takes one more step, switching the machine off.

== Decision

An Operator connects or disconnects the supply of a Pod only when the machine is switched off at its main switch.
The USB cable of a Pod may be replugged while the machine runs, as SYS-002 allows.

== Alternatives Considered

=== Hot plugging of the Pod supply

An Operator connects or disconnects the supply of a Pod while the machine runs.

Rejected: it needs a supply plug that is safe to connect under load, and it is not designed how a Pod behaves when it gets power.

== Consequences

* **Good:** No supply conductor is connected or disconnected under load.
* **Good:** The supply plug of a Pod needs no rating for switching under load.
* **Bad:** Adding or removing a Pod interrupts the service, because the whole machine is off while its supply is changed.
* **Bad:** The rule is a procedure, and no part of the machine enforces it.

=== Effect on the Quality Goals

* xref:system::quality-goals.adoc#QG-01[QG-01]: Better, because the Operator never works on powered supply conductors.
* xref:system::quality-goals.adoc#QG-04[QG-04]: Worse, because the Operator switches the whole machine off to change the supply of one Pod.
