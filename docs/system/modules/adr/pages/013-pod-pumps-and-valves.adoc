== Context

A Pod holds 10 bottles (xref:mechanics::index.adoc[Mechanics overview page]).
A carbonated drink expands while it is pumped, and the pump then runs slower.

=== Decision Drivers

* **Pumps keep their speed:** a carbonated drink must not slow down the pump that moves it (QG-05).

== Quality Goals

* xref:system::quality-goals.adoc#QG-05[QG-05]: Carbonated drinks do not slow down the pumps, so a drink is not delayed by them.
* xref:system::quality-goals.adoc#QG-02[QG-02]: A bottle at the wrong kind of dispenser puts a wrong amount into a drink.

== Decision

A Pod has 10 dispensers, 8 pumps for still ingredients and 2 valves for carbonated drinks.

== Alternatives Considered

=== Only pumps

Every dispenser is a pump.

Rejected: a carbonated drink expands while it is pumped and slows the pump down.

== Consequences

* **Good:** Carbonated drinks do not slow down the pumps.
* **Bad:** A Pod has two kinds of dispensers, with their own calibration and service.
* **Bad:** Only 2 bottles of a Pod can hold a carbonated drink.
* **Bad:** An Operator must put each ingredient into a bottle at the right kind of dispenser, and a mix-up puts a wrong amount into a drink.

=== Effect on the Quality Goals

* xref:system::quality-goals.adoc#QG-05[QG-05]: Better, because carbonated drinks do not slow down the pumps.
* xref:system::quality-goals.adoc#QG-02[QG-02]: Worse, because a bottle at the wrong kind of dispenser puts a wrong amount into a drink.
