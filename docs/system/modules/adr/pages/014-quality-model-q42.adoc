== Context

The quality goals of the machine need a quality model, so that chapter 10 can break each goal down into measurable quality scenarios.
The Quality Goals page mapped each goal onto ISO/IEC 25010:2023.
That standard is a product quality model for ICT and software products.
It does not cover the physical side of a machine, such as food-contact materials, mechanical hazards or the physical access for service.
The machine is a mechatronic system of a Station, Pods and a Power Management Unit.
The arc42 quality model Q42 describes the properties of a whole system with nine tags: #efficient, #flexible, #maintainable, #operable, #reliable, #safe, #secure, #suitable and #usable (https://quality.arc42.org/articles/arc42-quality-model[arc42 quality model]).
A requirement may carry several of these tags.
The safety of the machine for CE rests on a risk assessment of its hazards and the measures against them, not on a quality model.

=== Decision Drivers

* **A model for the whole system:** the model must name the qualities of the hardware as well as of the software (QG-01, QG-02, QG-04).
* **Several qualities per requirement:** a scenario such as a safe state that survives a link loss serves more than one quality (QG-01, QG-03).
* **Safety where CE expects it:** the safety argument for CE comes from the risk assessment, and the quality model must not stand in for it (QG-01, QG-02).

== Quality Goals

* xref:system::quality-goals.adoc#QG-01[QG-01]: #safe covers states that endanger people, and the risk assessment carries the CE argument.
* xref:system::quality-goals.adoc#QG-02[QG-02]: #safe and #suitable cover a wrong ingredient or amount, and the risk assessment covers the food-contact materials.
* xref:system::quality-goals.adoc#QG-04[QG-04]: #operable names the operating and servicing of the machine directly.

== Decision

The quality goals and the quality scenarios use the tags of the arc42 quality model Q42.
Each goal names one or more Q42 tags, and each quality scenario carries at least one tag of its goal.
The safety and food-safety scenarios also trace to the hazards and safety measures of the risk assessment once it exists.

== Alternatives Considered

=== ISO/IEC 25010:2023

Each goal maps onto a characteristic and subcharacteristics of ISO/IEC 25010:2023.

Rejected: the standard covers ICT and software products, so the physical qualities of the machine fall outside it, and one characteristic per goal does not fit scenarios that serve several qualities.

=== An own quality model

The project extends ISO/IEC 25010:2023 with characteristics for the hardware.

Rejected: it takes the most effort and has no outside reference.

== Consequences

* **Good:** The tags name the qualities of the whole machine, the hardware included.
* **Good:** A scenario can carry several tags without a forced fit.
* **Bad:** Q42 is young, it was published in 2023, and it is not an ISO standard.
* **Bad:** Readers who know ISO/IEC 25010 have to learn the Q42 tags.

=== Effect on the Quality Goals

* xref:system::quality-goals.adoc#QG-01[QG-01]: Better, because the safety argument rests on the risk assessment and #safe covers the whole machine.
* xref:system::quality-goals.adoc#QG-02[QG-02]: Better, because #safe and #suitable cover a wrong ingredient or amount, and the materials go to the risk assessment.
* xref:system::quality-goals.adoc#QG-04[QG-04]: Better, because #operable fits the servicing of the machine.
