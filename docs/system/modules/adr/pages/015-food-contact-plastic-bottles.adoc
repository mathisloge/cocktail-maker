== Context

A Pod holds each ingredient in a bottle that stands on its own load cell.
The Pods now stand hidden from the Guests, so the look of a bottle does not matter, only its function, size and price.
The bottle set of the Mechanics design (xref:mechanics::index.adoc[Mechanics overview page]) is a 1 l glass milk bottle.
A design round with eight such bottles for the still ingredients, apart from the carbonated bottles of xref:013-pod-pumps-and-valves.adoc[SYS-013], gives a Pod of about 880 × 670 × 570 mm with 8 l of ingredients.
Eight square 2 l HDPE wide-neck bottles give a Pod of about 880 × 340 × 520 mm with 16 l.
An Operator refills a bottle from the top or lifts it out to refill, empty or rinse it, and a glass bottle breaks when it hits an edge.

xref:009-wetted-parts.adoc[SYS-009] allowed only tubes and stainless steel parts to touch a liquid.
It did not name the bottle at all, so it did not even cover the glass bottle.
This ADR supersedes SYS-009.

The EU rules for plastics in contact with food are EU 1935/2004 and EU 10/2011.
EU 10/2011 tests a plastic with food simulants.
Simulant D1 (50 % ethanol) is used for alcoholic foods above 20 %, simulant C for beverages of 6 to 20 % vol, and simulants A and B for aqueous and acidic foods such as juices.
A contact of more than 1 day and up to 3 days is tested for 3 days, and a contact between 20 °C and 40 °C is tested at 40 °C.
No simulant exists for spirits above 50 % vol.

=== Decision Drivers

* **Food-safe bottles:** a bottle touches every ingredient, so its material must be food-safe and the proof must be checkable (QG-02).
* **Safe handling:** an Operator lifts a bottle out of the Pod, and a broken bottle must not cut the Operator or spill an ingredient (QG-04).
* **Free choice for printed parts:** the material of a printed part follows its mechanical needs, as in SYS-009 (QG-06).

== Quality Goals

* xref:system::quality-goals.adoc#QG-02[QG-02]: Every part that touches an ingredient is a food-grade tube, a stainless steel part, glass, or a plastic part with a declaration that covers the ingredients and the contact time.
* xref:system::quality-goals.adoc#QG-04[QG-04]: An Operator lifts a bottle out without the risk of breaking it, and empties and rinses it after at most 48 h.
* xref:system::quality-requirements.adoc#QG-06[QG-06]: No printed part touches a liquid, so printed parts still change material and shape without a food-safety review.
* xref:system::quality-requirements.adoc#QS-09[QS-09]: The parts along the flow path are counted against the list of this ADR.

== Decision

These parts may touch an ingredient or a drink: food-grade tubes, stainless steel parts, glass, and HDPE or PP.
HDPE and PP are allowed for bottles, caps and fittings, and not for tubes.
An HDPE or PP part needs a declaration of compliance under EU 1935/2004 and EU 10/2011.
The declaration covers the food simulants A, B, C and D1, or all food types, at least 3 days at 40 °C, and repeated use.
No 3D printed part touches a liquid.
An ingredient stays in a bottle for at most 48 h, and then the Operator empties and rinses the bottle.
Each bottle holds one ingredient, and an Operator replaces a bottle that smells or is worn.

== Alternatives Considered

=== Glass bottle sets as hidden bottles

The 1 l glass bottle sets stay as the hidden bottles of a Pod.

Rejected: the Pod is twice as deep and holds half the volume, and a glass bottle breaks when an Operator lifts it out.

=== Larger glass bottles

Glass bottles of 1.5 to 3 l replace the bottle sets.

Rejected: they need a new bottle, holder and dip tube, and they break when an Operator lifts them out.

=== Polycarbonate

Clear polycarbonate gastronomy tubs hold the ingredients.

Rejected: polycarbonate can craze with alcohol and cleaners, and it may contain bisphenol A.

=== PET

PET bottles hold the ingredients.

Rejected: PET is fine for short contact, but it is not dishwasher safe.

=== Bag-in-box

Bags in boxes hold the ingredients.

Rejected: the bags are single use, and standard film is only rated to about 17 % alcohol.

=== HDPE and PP without a time limit

Ingredients stay in an HDPE or PP bottle for as long as an Operator wants.

Rejected: the declaration then needs tests of 10 days and longer, and ethanol loss through the wall and stress cracking start to matter.

=== Bottles with an outlet at the bottom

Each bottle has an outlet with a quick coupling at the bottom, as in some commercial drink dispensers.

Rejected: liquid always stands at the coupling, and a leak empties the bottle onto the load cell and toward the electronics (xref:system::contracts.adoc#IF-07-R04[IF-07-R04]).

== Consequences

* **Good:** A Pod is half as deep and holds twice the volume.
* **Good:** A bottle does not break when an Operator lifts it out or drops it.
* **Good:** The food-safety argument names every material that touches a liquid, including the bottle.
* **Good:** PP fittings with a declaration are allowed, so the barbed fittings can be plastic.
* **Bad:** No candidate bottle has a published declaration that covers alcohol today, so Mechanics must ask the makers for one.
* **Bad:** HDPE takes up strong aromas such as anethole or limonene, so a bottle serves one ingredient only and is replaced when it smells or is worn.
* **Bad:** The 48 h limit adds a step for the Operator, at the latest after 48 h.
* **Bad:** Spirits above 50 % vol are covered by no release of the tube or the bottle, because simulant D1 is 50 % ethanol.
The risk is small at 48 h of contact, but it stays open.

=== Effect on the Quality Goals

* xref:system::quality-goals.adoc#QG-02[QG-02]: Neutral.
The list of allowed materials grows, but every new material needs a declaration that covers the ingredients and the contact time.
* xref:system::quality-goals.adoc#QG-04[QG-04]: Better and worse.
A bottle does not break, but the Operator empties and rinses every bottle after at most 48 h.
* xref:system::quality-requirements.adoc#QG-06[QG-06]: Neutral, because no printed part touches a liquid, as before.
* xref:system::quality-requirements.adoc#QS-09[QS-09]: Neutral, because the count now checks the declaration of each plastic part as well.
