== Context

xref:008-enclosure-colours.adoc[SYS-008] made the outside of the Station and the Pods follow the colours of the UI palette, because a Guest saw both next to the touch display.
Since xref:016-station-show-front.adoc[SYS-016] the Pods stand hidden wherever there is room, and the Guest sees only the Station with its show front.
The parts are printed on a Prusa Core One with INDX, and each colour change in a print costs time and purge material.
This ADR supersedes SYS-008.

=== Decision Drivers

* **A good look:** the screen, the Station and the show front should look like one product (QG-05).
* **Food-safe flow path:** a colour must never bring a material into the flow path that is not food-safe (QG-02).
* **Cheap Pods:** a hidden part should not pay for a look that nobody sees (QG-04).

== Quality Goals

* xref:system::quality-goals.adoc#QG-05[QG-05]: The Station and the show front repeat the colours of the UI, so the machine looks like one product to the Guest.
* xref:system::quality-goals.adoc#QG-02[QG-02]: The rule covers only the outside, so the parts in the flow path keep their food-safe material.
* xref:system::quality-goals.adoc#QG-04[QG-04]: A Pod is printed in any colour, so a spare part is quick to print.

== Decision

The outside of the Station and its show front follows the colours of the UI palette where possible.
The Pods are free in colour.

== Alternatives Considered

=== Pods keep the palette

The rule of SYS-008 stays for the Pods, although they are hidden.

Rejected: it costs print time and purge material for a look that no Guest sees.

=== A neutral outside

All outside parts are black or grey, independent of the UI.

Rejected: it is simple, but the Station would not match the warm colours of the screen.

== Consequences

* **Good:** The screen, the Station and the show front look like one product.
* **Good:** A Pod is printed in one colour, which saves time and purge material.
* **Bad:** A filament never matches a palette colour exactly, so the Station only comes close to the screen.
* **Bad:** A change of a UI colour can make printed parts of the Station outdated.
* **Bad:** A Pod that stands in view of the Guests after all does not match the Station.

=== Effect on the Quality Goals

* xref:system::quality-goals.adoc#QG-05[QG-05]: Neutral, because the Guest sees only the Station, and it keeps the palette.
* xref:system::quality-goals.adoc#QG-02[QG-02]: Neutral, because the parts in the flow path are outside the rule.
* xref:system::quality-goals.adoc#QG-04[QG-04]: Better, because a spare part of a Pod needs no colour.
